The first question should be: does the right tool already exist?

I have built technology systems myself, so I understand the real cost of custom development — not just the build, but maintenance, integration, training, and the risk that a better tool appears six months later.

Today, powerful AI products are appearing faster than most business owners can evaluate them. My job is to stay close to that market, understand what the tools actually do, and match useful technology to real business problems.

Which tool is actually right? Will it fit your workflow? Is the price justified? Can your team use it? Is there already a better option than building from scratch?

My bias is simple: find and deploy before you build. Custom work is the exception, not the product.
